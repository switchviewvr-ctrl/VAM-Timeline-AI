# Prompt To Interaction Plan

Prompt planning now includes pose, partner relation, and contact/support.

Example:

`slow cowgirl grinding, leaning forward, hands on partner chest`

maps to:

- family: cowgirl
- actor role: rider
- partner role: receiver
- pose subtype: cowgirl_lean_forward_supported
- motion subtype: oval_grind
- coordinate frame: partner_pelvis_local
- contact targets: lHand and rHand to partner.chest
- constraints: keep pelvis aligned to partner and hands near partner chest

This is still a rule-based planning prototype, not final text-to-animation.
