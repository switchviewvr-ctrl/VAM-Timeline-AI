# Why Retrieval Is Not Final Generation

Primitive retrieval is used to inspect the learned primitive space. It answers questions like:

- Which abstract primitive groups match this semantic plan?
- Are there enough examples for the requested subtype?
- What rhythm and amplitude ranges exist in the data?

It does not concatenate old Timeline clips. A future generator must synthesize new relative controller tracks from primitive parameters, validate anchors and controller plausibility, then retarget safely to the current VaM person.

Review exports remain for inspection only. They are not generation-safe templates unless explicitly validated by a later retargeting/export stage.
