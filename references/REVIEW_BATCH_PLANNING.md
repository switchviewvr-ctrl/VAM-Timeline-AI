# Review Batch Planning

The larger review batch planner creates a plan only. It does not export a
60-item package until explicitly requested.

Planned mix:

- 20 clean Cowgirl generation-safe candidates
- 10 Cowgirl contact/support candidates
- 8 Cowgirl pose-context or intro/alignment candidates
- 8 BJ/oral candidates
- 5 receiver-response candidates
- 5 standing hand/head candidates
- 4 unknown/unusable candidates

Selection should enforce sample, scene, and near-duplicate caps. The goal is a
diverse human review set, not a large pile of repeated low-motion windows.
