# Partner-Relative Interaction Semantics

Cowgirl generation is partner-relative, not world-relative. The actor is the
rider, the partner is the receiver/support target, and relations such as
`rider_above_partner`, `pelvis_aligned`, and `receiver_lying_on_back` are
tracked independently from motion.

If pair context is missing, contact/support remains unknown instead of being
invented from hand motion alone.
