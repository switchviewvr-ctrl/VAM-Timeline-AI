# Cowgirl Lean-back Supported Pose

`cowgirl_lean_back_supported` is a valid frontal Cowgirl pose subtype.

The rider leans her torso backward while staying in a front-facing Cowgirl relationship. Her hands are behind her body and may support against the partner legs/thighs or another behind-body support surface.

This is not reverse Cowgirl by default. Reverse Cowgirl requires explicit facing-away/orientation evidence, not merely a backward torso lean.

Audit labels and review batches using this subtype are calibration material only. They are not manual training truth and are not merged into `manual_labels.yaml`.
